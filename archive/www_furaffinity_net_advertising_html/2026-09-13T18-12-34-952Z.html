Advertising on FA

Updated - 02/27/2026

Our community-based advertising delivers over a billion impressions each month! In addition to our third party ads, these ads help cover the costs of hosting and paying for site development.

How Do I Advertise on FA?

Step One - Send an e-mail to advertising@furaffinity.net, including your website address, a brief description of your site, and your completed ad banners as an attachment. If your advertisement is date/time sensitive, please be sure to note that in your initial e-mail. Please expect up to 3 business days (M-F) for a response.

Step Two - Advertising banners and links will be reviewed for both content and quality. On rare occasions, ads may be rejected and sent back with feedback as to why they were declined (after all, we want your ad to be successful!).

Step Three - Once your ad is approved, further instructions will be sent via e-mail. Fur Affinity only accepts payment via Paypal.

If you have any questions or concerns, please email advertising@furaffinity.net.
