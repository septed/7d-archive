So one of my jobs is afterschool, meaning it only happens during the school year. However they also do a summer camp. At the beginning of summer I had a conversation with my boss about what the reality of summer and next year would look like as I was barely making ends meet with my 2 jobs. She all but promised me more hours during the summer and the fall. However, after giving me about 30ish hours in the first 4 weeks of summer, she basically forgot about me (Or is punishing me? After giving me a shift via email that we both forgot about) and I have had no income from that job in the past month. With rent coming up this is really starting to stress me out, even if I worked tomorrow tho I wouldn’t get that check for 2 weeks. I just have really tried to be a team player for this place, and it feels like when things got busy I got tossed to the wayside. All of this to say, I was considering looking at new jobs but I didn’t want to leave her high and dry for the school year, but at this point idk if it feels worth it to keep chasing a job where I don’t even completely feel fulfilled.
