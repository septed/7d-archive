Violet & Marlowe — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Atrix Digital Media Inc., doing business as Wesley Wang Media (“Atrix,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), a Delaware corporation, collects, uses, and shares information when you visit violetandmarlowe.com (the “Site”) for Violet & Marlowe, a Wesley Wang Media production, call the Violet & Marlowe fan hotline at (339) 330-4776 (the “Hotline”), participate in the Violet & Marlowe Alerts text message program (the “Text Program”), or otherwise interact with us (together, the “Services”).

This Policy does not cover third-party sites or platforms we link to, such as YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, or any investment platform, which are governed by their own policies.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Text Program: the mobile phone number you text from, the keyword you send, the date and time of your opt-in and any opt-out, and the content of any messages you send us.
  • Hotline: the phone number you call from, the date, time, and duration of your call, and your progress through the Hotline, including the keys you press and any spoken responses the system is designed to recognize. Hotline calls are not recorded.
  • Forms and contact: your name, email address, and anything else you choose to submit through a form on the Site (including forms hosted on Google Forms) or when you email us.

Information collected automatically

When you use the Site, we and our service providers may collect standard log and device information such as IP address, browser type, pages viewed, referring pages, and approximate location derived from IP address, using cookies and similar technologies. See Section 6.

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate the Text Program, including sending the messages you opted in to receive and honoring opt-out requests.
  • Operate the Hotline, route calls through the experience, and track how far callers progress.
  • Contact you individually about your participation in the Hotline or Services, for example to follow up with callers who complete the Hotline. Outreach to a phone number that has not opted in to the Text Program is made by a real person, not by automated messaging.
  • Process form submissions and respond to your questions and support requests.
  • Understand how people use the Site and Hotline so we can improve them.
  • Protect against fraud, abuse, and misuse of the Services.
  • Comply with law and enforce our terms.

3. How we share information

We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers that process information on our behalf and only for the purposes above, including Twilio (telephony and text messaging), Lovable and Shopify (website platform and analytics), and Google (forms).
  • Legal reasons, when required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Atrix, our fans, or others.
  • Business transfers, if Atrix or the Violet & Marlowe property is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, in which case information may be transferred subject to this Policy.
  • With your direction or consent.

We do not sell your personal information.

Text messaging opt-in data

Your mobile phone number, text messaging opt-in consent, and related Text Program data will not be shared with or sold to any third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. The sharing categories above exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent, which are used only to operate the Text Program.

4. Text Program details

  • Opt in: Text REBELLION to (339) 330-4776.
  • Frequency: Message frequency varies. You may receive up to 6 messages per month.
  • Cost: Message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: Reply STOP at any time.
  • Help: Reply HELP, email team@wesleywangmedia.com, or visit https://violetandmarlowe.com/hotline.
  • Terms: https://violetandmarlowe.com/hotline/terms

Consent to receive text messages is never required to make a purchase or to use any part of the Site or Hotline.

5. Data retention

We keep Text Program opt-in and opt-out records for at least four years after your last interaction so that we can honor your preferences and demonstrate consent as required by law. Hotline call logs, including caller numbers and progress data, are kept for 24 months unless needed longer for legal reasons. Form submissions are kept for as long as the related program is active and for a reasonable period afterward. We delete or de-identify information when it is no longer needed.

6. Cookies and analytics

The Site is built and hosted on Lovable and may use Shopify and similar platform tools to measure traffic and engagement. These tools use cookies and similar technologies for basic functionality and analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect some Site features. We do not use cookies to serve third-party targeted advertising.

7. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The Text Program is limited to people 18 and older. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us at team@wesleywangmedia.com and we will delete it.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Text messages: reply STOP at any time.
  • Email: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or contact us.
  • Access, correction, deletion: request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing team@wesleywangmedia.com. We will verify your request and respond within the time required by applicable law.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, including the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion, to correct inaccurate information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising your rights. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. To exercise these rights, contact us at team@wesleywangmedia.com. You may designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information transmitted over the internet or mobile networks.

10. Users outside the United States

The Services are operated from the United States and intended for U.S. audiences. If you access them from elsewhere, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version at https://violetandmarlowe.com/hotline/privacy with a new effective date. Material changes may be announced through the Site or the Text Program.
