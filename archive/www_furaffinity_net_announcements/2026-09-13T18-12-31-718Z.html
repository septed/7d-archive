June 15th, 2026- Site updates, API, Beta, and Downtime!
News & Updates | General | Posted 3 months ago
Hey there Fuzzies!

It's been a while! Rednef has been having a lot of fun while I’ve been hard at work alongside the Tech staff. But fret not, we have a big juicy update for all of you!

Announcement Page New Notification Type (Modern only)



We’ve been posting updates and banners more often which means there’s been way more journal posts. As a result, we’re finding there are times where journal content would overlap and Rednef and I have to decide who gets to post. We don’t want to cut any announcement time short, so for these cases we are introducing the new announcement page and announcement notifications!



The new page includes all announcement journals by our mascots, Fender (Site and Tech Updates) and Rednef (Community Events and Spotlight). Announcement journals are sorted in chronological order and any you haven’t seen or marked as read will be highlighted. We hope this makes it easier for you all to keep up-to-date and easily see any journals you may have missed, including community events, site updates, and more! On our end, this means we don’t have to wait to share exciting things as they come up. We don't expect the number of announcements per month to increase, mind you. This just gives us the flexibility to share information with you in a more timely fashion without being confined to rigid schedules.

External Link Filtering



We all know that not everyone online plays nice. Unfortunately there are people who try to use Fur Affinity as a tool to take advantage of others. To address potential phishing and scam attempts via links, we've implemented a new intermediate page that will alert the user when they're leaving the Fur Affinity website. This will ideally cut down on accidental link clicks or situations where a scammer might try to mask the actual intent of their website.

We’ve also noticed some scammers sending notes impersonating staff. If you ever get a note that says it’s from Fur Affinity, verify that the person you're talking to is an actual Fur Affinity staff account before following any instructions! Staff accounts will have this shield icon before their name. We will NEVER ask you for your password or direct you off-site. If you encounter any suspicious activity, please open a Trouble Ticket to report it.

Comment Management (beta.furaffinity.net)



With recent adjustments to notifications and allowing for shouts to be hidden, we're adding the highly requested comment management page! This page will show ALL comments made by you, enabling you to delete or locate your comments at a glance. The comment management page is available for testing right now on the beta version of FA. You can access it via this link. Like other beta deployments, this is only available to FA+ members for testing at this time and it will go live for all users after testing is complete. If you want to give it a whirl before it’s officially released, sign up for FA+!

Bot and Third Party Activity


We understand that some people use bots and other third party software (such as art archivers, etc.) to interact with Fur Affinity. Due to the increase in traffic and the popularity of these solutions, we are updating our policy with regards to third party software. This section does NOT apply to individuals using software to interact with Fur Affinity in a more streamlined way like Postybirb and FABUI (these are not official Fur Affinity apps, but ones we currently permit).

We request that you follow these guidelines when using or developing third party bots and apps:
--Limit bot activity to periods with less than 15k users online.
--Keep your requests to once per second.
--Employ a proper exponential backoff scheme to mitigate overwhelming our services.
--During DDoS attacks, Cloudflare’s DDoS Mitigation may impact the use of third party software. We kindly ask that you do not use third party software while DDoS mitigation is underway.
--All third party apps interacting with Fur Affinity are the responsibility of their creators and are not supported in any way by the Fur Affinity Team. We will not take bug reports or handle issues with regard to how these tools interact with Fur Affinity.
--If your service interacts with Fur Affinity as a Machine-to-Machine service for the purposes of scraping or verification, we require that you seek permission to access our services by filing a Trouble Ticket in the Tech Category under “Access Requests” so we can identify your traffic pattern and ensure you retain access.
--We reserve the right at any time to revoke or block access based on perceived abuse patterns or unidentified, strenuous traffic. Please be respectful!

We’ve also received reports of users' scripts breaking as a result of Cloudflare blocks. We will be reaching out to those individuals who have submitted tickets to us shortly, and we apologize for the wait while we work on our internals to better support third party interactions in the future!

An Official Fur Affinity API and Closed Beta!



As part of the Developer Friendly Initiative, we will soon be releasing a consumer-facing API to interact with Fur Affinity programmatically. We will reach out to pre-selected individuals for testing soon, followed by an invite-only closed beta for interested parties, then a rolling release schedule from there. Anyone can apply to the API closed beta, and FA+ members will be able to join directly from the Discord once the beta goes live.

If you are interested in participating in the API closed beta when it is ready, please fill out this form: https://forms.gle/8XNUo61fK4VyQdHA6

We plan to offer a read-only API for the time being while we learn about the API’s impact on our database and begin tweaking to allow for writes in the future. We appreciate everyone’s understanding as we slowly implement new changes to a very old codebase!

Right now we are facilitating access to the API through Discord as a way to better collect feedback and let the community interact with the development team, but this will not be the case for the final release of the API! The final release of the API will allow you to interact with and register for the API directly through the site.

More changes!



Beyond these changes there are other smaller, but still important, adjustments to the website which you can view here.

Upcoming Downtime



We have a planned maintenance coming up for June 22, 2026 between 05:00:00 AM to 01:00:00 PM. This will allow us to make various improvements on our backend including upgrades to our database.

Thank you!


We have been very hard at work behind the scenes working on improving our Continuous Integration and Deployment Pipelines, as well as finally moving our version control to Git and creating reproducible environments for Fur Affinity. It’s been a lot of hard work from a small number of talented and dedicated community members that stepped up as staff, and we want to continue improving Fur Affinity for everyone. This also means that in the coming months, we’ll be able to begin supporting more developers and more volunteers as our codebase becomes more modernized and less fragile, so be on the lookout for recruitments! We are looking forward to working with the community and making something incredible together for everyone!

Reminder to keep comments on top and be excellent to each other!

March 23rd, 2026- Notification Purge and Downtime
News & Updates | General | Posted 5 months ago
Hey there, Fuzzies!

We hate to have to make a post like this but we’re sure some of you noticed some issues with your notifications this morning. Back on July 18th, 2019 we had announced we’d be autoclearing notifications. We didn’t end up clearing them as aggressively as mentioned in that journal, however with time it became apparent we needed to. So on May 4th, 2025 we announced that we were planning to start auto-clearing notifications that are older than 6 months. We’ve been working in the background to prepare for some upgrades and downtime to get this done, but we never intended for this to be pushed live before giving another announcement so everyone would have a heads up that it was going to start.

Our tech team was discussing this purge during their most recent meeting. It was decided that we’d announce the downtime in this journal, then purge the notifications during that scheduled downtime. Unfortunately, due to a miscommunication, the pruning process was mistakenly started much earlier at 2:00 AM ET this morning and ended at 9:00 AM ET when staff was alerted by our community.

Because of how large these tables are, we cannot estimate how many notifications have been removed, nor can we restore this data. We have, however, been able to determine the cutoff date of where notifications were cleared.

Any notification older than the below are no longer in the database (all times in ET):
Journal comments: September 24, 2025 9:11 AM
Submission comments: November 23, 2018 10:26 PM
Favorites: January 30, 2013 9:02 AM
Journals: September 24, 2009 4:37 AM
Shouts: September 24, 2025 9:08 AM
Submissions: August 9, 2018 11:57 AM
Watches: August 24, 2016 6:49 AM

To be clear, it is only the notifications that have been removed. All the actual comments, favorites, journals, shouts, submissions, and watches are still there.

We have discussed the miscommunication with our team to ensure something like this will not happen again. We apologize profusely for any inconvenience this may have caused. Internet Safety and Scamming



We want to address some concerns with internet safety and scamming, especially with the upcoming holidays which typically have an increase in suspicious and fraudulent activities.

Whether you use Fur Affinity, Discord, Bluesky, Reddit, Telegram or any other form of social media, furries by and large have been seeing a rise in attempts for commission and art scams. While our moderation team works behind the scenes to stop them, the only way to truly stop scammers is with the knowledge of their existence and tactics. We have seen a very small, but persistent group continue to try to cause issues, and we want to remind our users of some safety best practices.

Common Examples of Scams



– Staff Impersonation Scams: We have recently seen an increase in scammers pretending to be site staff. Fur Affinity staff will never ask you to change your email to something that is not yours, nor will we ask you to give us your password. In addition, our staff will not contact you about matters related to the website or your account in the Fur Affinity Discord or as a private message on Discord. Legitimate notifications from Fur Affinity Staff will always appear in one of two forms:
– Notes on site from the official official "Fur Affinity Staff" account (look for the star shield icon before the display name, like the one next to my name, and double-check the account name).

– An email from an [at]furaffinity.net address.

Fur Affinity staff will never...
– Contact you and ask you to change your password.
– Contact you and ask you to change your email to a different email which you do not own.
– Discuss an action or possible action for a user through a third party.
– Contact you through another platform [Discord, Twitter, Bluesky] about an issue occurring on our site.
– Contact you as an individual staff member in a direct message (DM) to you on Discord, another platform, or on our site.

Users with questions, comments, or concerns about moderation actions should open a Trouble Ticket or send an email via appeals.

– YCH Scams:
One of the more common scams is the claim that an older YCH (“your character here”) commission has suddenly become available again after the original buyer backed out. They target previous highest bidders on YCHs in an attempt to dupe them into paying. Most of the time, they use new accounts with a name that is very close to the original artist’s username, but the spelling/formatting is just slightly off.

– Commission Scammers:
If someone messages you first with an unsolicited commission offer, that is a red flag. Scammers love to impersonate existing artists via stolen or AI-generated content to lure people in with the promise of affordable, high-quality commissions with fast turnaround times. They then send out mass notices in an attempt to catch as many people as they can. This type of scammer will often request for further communication to continue on another platform, such as Discord or Telegram. If somebody messages first, always be wary and do your research before committing to a commission.

– "Accidentally Reported You" Scams:
Another common tactic is the scammer pretending they've reported your account, then telling you what to do to make sure action isn't taken against you. The directions and links they give are malicious and should not be followed. If you're ever unsure about the status of your account please open a Trouble Ticket and staff will assist you.

Protecting Yourself



– Invoice When Paying:
Always ask for an invoice, and NEVER pay with “friends and family” options unless you know and trust the person. Doing so waives your rights to a refund and any money sent is considered a non-refundable gift. Again, always request an invoice; they come with buyer protections built in.

– Double-Check Their Profile:
Take a moment to double-check that the person you are messaging is who they say they are. New accounts will have a green leaf icon in place after their display name.



Ensure they are not using a new account, and their profile takes you to a user page that has been registered for a while and has an established gallery. Galleries without a visible history of completed works can be a red flag. Be cautious if you receive a message from an account you do not know or appears new, especially if you get an unfamiliar link encouraging you to click it.

– Double-Check Their Username:
While display names can have duplicates, usernames cannot. Make sure to check the username, not just the display name!
